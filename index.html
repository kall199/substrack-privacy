Privacy Policy for Substrack

Effective Date: September 13, 2026
Last Updated: September 13, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Substrack ("we", "our", "the app"). We built Substrack to help you track your recurring subscriptions in one place. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have over your data.

We take privacy seriously. This policy is written in plain language so you understand exactly what happens with your data.

What Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign up, we collect:

  • Your email address
  • A password (stored securely, encrypted, never in plain text)
  • Your display name (optional)

Subscription Data

When you add subscriptions to Substrack, we store:

  • Subscription name and category
  • Amount and currency
  • Billing cycle (weekly, monthly, quarterly, yearly)
  • Next billing date
  • Whether it's a free trial
  • Whether it's paused
  • Renewal history (dates and amounts of past renewals)

Preferences

We save your app settings including:

  • Default currency
  • Notification preferences (how many days before renewal to notify you)

Device Information

We use your device to send local notifications about upcoming renewals. These notifications are generated and delivered by your device — we do not track when you view them.

What We Do NOT Collect

To be clear about what we don't do:

  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, or files
  • We do not use analytics or tracking services
  • We do not track your behavior across other apps or websites
  • We do not sell your data to anyone
  • We do not use your data for advertising
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or marketing companies

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only to:

  • Provide the core Substrack service (tracking your subscriptions)
  • Send you renewal reminders you configured
  • Convert currencies using live exchange rates
  • Authenticate you when you log in
  • Respond to your questions or support requests

Third-Party Services We Use

Substrack uses a small number of third-party services to function. Here's exactly what they do:

Supabase (Authentication & Database)

Supabase stores your account and subscription data on secure cloud servers. When you sign up, log in, or add subscriptions, that data goes to Supabase. Supabase has its own privacy policy available at https://supabase.com/privacy.

ExchangeRate-API (Currency Conversion)

When we need to convert one currency to another, we fetch current exchange rates from exchangerate-api.com. We only send them our request for rates — we do not send any of your personal information. Their privacy policy is at https://www.exchangerate-api.com/privacy.

Expo (Push Notifications and App Infrastructure)

Substrack is built with Expo, which provides infrastructure for delivering notifications. Local notifications about your renewals are scheduled and delivered by your device, not by us or Expo.

Apple (App Distribution)

If you downloaded Substrack from the App Store, Apple collected your download and may share aggregate download statistics with us. Their privacy policy is available at https://www.apple.com/privacy.

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase's secure infrastructure, which runs on Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Singapore. All connections between the app and Supabase are encrypted using industry-standard TLS/HTTPS.

Your password is hashed using bcrypt before storage. We never see or store your plain-text password.

Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your data:

View Your Data

All your data is visible to you in the app at any time.

Update Your Data

You can edit any subscription, change your display name, currency preference, and notification settings directly in the app.

Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time by going to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete my account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone. When you delete your account, we remove:

  • Your email address and login credentials
  • All your subscription data
  • All renewal history
  • All settings and preferences

There is no waiting period and no way to recover deleted data.

Export Your Data

If you would like a copy of your data before deleting, contact us at the email below and we'll send you an export.

Children's Privacy

Substrack is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us and we will delete the information.

Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your data is deleted immediately and permanently.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make significant changes, we'll notify you within the app or by email before the changes take effect. The "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy always reflects the most recent version.

International Users

Substrack is available worldwide. By using the app, you consent to your data being processed on servers located in Singapore (where our Supabase instance is hosted) or other jurisdictions where our service providers operate.

Legal Basis for Processing (For Users in the EU/UK)

If you are in the European Union or United Kingdom, we process your data based on:

  • Contract: to provide the Substrack service you signed up for
  • Consent: for optional features like notifications
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the app secure and functional

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data at any time.
